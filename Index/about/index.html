How to place an order

Click the "Buy Now" Button – When you find a product you like, simply click the "Buy Now" button.

Redirect to WhatsApp – You will be redirected to WhatsApp, where the product details will be sent automatically.

Confirm Your Order – Our team will review your request and confirm the order with pricing, availability, and payment details.

Complete the Payment – Once confirmed, proceed with the payment using the available options.

Get Your Order Delivered – Your order will be shipped, and tracking details will be provided for easy monitoring.

At Trend Mart, we ensure a seamless and convenient shopping experience. Start shopping now and upgrade your style!

Note: Payment is accepted only via UPI, and all UPI options are available. Cash on Delivery not Available.

Terms & Condition

We are not responsible for product delivery as we hand over the shipment to courier services. Delivery is managed by the respective courier teams, but in case of any issues, we will fully support you to ensure successful delivery. Rest assured, your product will be delivered. We commonly use DTDC and Blue Dart for shipping. If any issues arise with these couriers, we will arrange an alternative courier for your order.

Note: Orders cannot be canceled once they are processed and shipped.

Return policy

At Trend Mart, we do not accept returns or exchanges after an order has been shipped; however, if you receive an incorrect or damaged item, please contact our customer support via WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery and provide an unedited video that captures the entire unboxing process—including a clear view of the unopened package with the shipping label and your address, as well as a 360-degree view of the product clearly showing any damage—so that our team can verify your claim and arrange for a replacement or suitable solution.

Note: Otherwise no return strictly.
